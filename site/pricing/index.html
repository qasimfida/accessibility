What does free include?

The MIT license permits commercial use and modification while requiring its copyright and license notice. Hosting, integration work and accessibility testing are your responsibility. No managed hosting, guaranteed response time or paid support service is offered.

Do I need a paid plan to be accessible?

There are no paid plans. A widget helps visitors personalise a page; it does not make your website conformant by itself. Accessible content, design, development and testing remain essential.
