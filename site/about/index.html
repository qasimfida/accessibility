Accessiblit / about

A small toolkit. A considered purpose.

Made for personal choice

People read the web differently.

Accessiblit gives visitors control over text, colour and navigation without asking them to create an account. Eighteen tools, one small integration and preferences that stay in their browser.

Less infrastructure. More independence.

Open by design

The toolkit is free under the MIT license. Read the source, self-host a build and adapt it to your project.

Explore the source

Honest about its role

Personalisation can make a page more comfortable. It does not fix missing labels, inaccessible flows or the underlying site's conformance.
