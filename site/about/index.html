Our purpose

More room for personal choice.

People read, move and focus in different ways. Accessiblit gives visitors eighteen practical controls for text, colour and navigation, with preferences that stay in their browser.

Our approach

Small by design.
Open by default.

One script or a typed import. No account to create, no tracking to opt out of and no runtime services to depend on. Integrate once and let visitors choose the combination that feels comfortable.

Explore the documentation

Built with clear intentions

What matters in the details.

Visitor choice

Controls belong to the person using the page. Reset returns them to the original presentation.

Local by design

No cookies, analytics or runtime requests. Blocked storage falls back to memory.

Honest boundaries

Personalisation complements accessible foundations. It does not replace semantic content, keyboard design or testing.
